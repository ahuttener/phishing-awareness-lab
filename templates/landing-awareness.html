If this were real, you'd just have been phished.

This page is the end of a controlled, self-targeted phishing simulation. No account was touched and nothing was compromised. The point is to recognise the signals next time.

🚩 Red flags in the email you clicked

  • Urgency: "within 24 hours or access will be suspended" — pressure to act fast.
  • Mismatch: a friendly display name hiding a sending address that doesn't match the real domain.
  • Generic hook: "a new sign-in was detected" — a lure that fits almost anyone.
  • Link, not navigation: you were asked to click, not to open the site yourself.

✅ What to do instead

  • Don't click. Open the service yourself from a bookmark or by typing the address.
  • Check the real sender — expand the address, not just the display name.
  • Turn on phishing-resistant MFA (passkeys / FIDO2) so a stolen password isn't enough.
  • Report it to your security team / provider.
